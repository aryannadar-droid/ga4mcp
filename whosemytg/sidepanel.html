Under the hoodHow it thinks ▾
  1. Reads the site. The page in your tab as you see it, plus a few more pages it picks from your links (About, Products, Pricing, FAQ...). It skips login, cart and legal pages. Each page gets a number.
  2. Sends one request to the provider you picked: the instructions below, plus the page text and your gossip. Nothing else, and only to that provider.
  3. The AI drafts 4–6 candidate TGs, defined by situation (what's true for them right now), not age or income.
  4. Merges lookalikes. If two would get the same ad, page or offer, they're one TG.
  5. Runs the overlap test. No real person should fit two TGs, so each one gets a yes/no "bouncer question".
  6. Rates buy-intent from 0 to 5 stars. Only one TG can be top.
  7. Writes the cards: 5 reasons to buy, each tagged and citing page numbers, plus a lead line, offer, ad angle and landing-page hero.
  8. Follows house rules: no competitor names, no invented stats, prices or reviews, and risky claims go to "Run past the client".
